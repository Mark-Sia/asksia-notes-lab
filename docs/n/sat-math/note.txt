🧮 FORMULAS TO KNOW COLD · 必背公式
• Slope: m = (y₂ − y₁) / (x₂ − x₁)
• Slope-intercept · point-slope: y = mx + b  ·  y − y₁ = m(x − x₁)
• Parallel · perpendicular: equal slopes · negative-reciprocal slopes
• Quadratic formula: x = [−b ± √(b² − 4ac)] / 2a
• Discriminant: b² − 4ac  → >0 two roots · =0 one · <0 none
• Vertex form: y = a(x − h)² + k · axis x = −b/2a
• Zero-product rule: (x − p)(x − q) = 0 ⇒ x = p or q
• Exponential model: y = a(1 + r)ᵗ  (growth) · y = a(1 − r)ᵗ (decay)
• Systems: one solution · none (parallel) · infinite (same line)
• Inequalities: flip the sign ONLY when × or ÷ by a negative
• Word problems: Total = constant + rate × variable → solve → substitute back

🔑 KEY TERMS · 关键术语（双语）
• Balance principle（等式平衡原则）— whatever you do to one side, do to the other
• Multistage adaptive (MST)（多阶段自适应）— routed once per section, not per question
• Student-produced response（自填答案题）— type it: fractions/decimals ok · no %, $, commas
• Equivalent expressions（等价表达式）— a rewrite (distribute, FOIL, factor) — not something you solve
• Extraneous solution（增根）— appears after squaring/clearing fractions — always check back
• Operational question（计分题）— 90 of 98 count; 8 are unscored pretest items

⚠️ 8 TRAPS · 陷阱
1. Read what is ASKED: if it wants 6x − 3, solve for 6x − 3 directly, not x.
2. SPR answers: no percent sign, dollar sign or commas. 3/4 or 0.75 both fine.
3. Squared both sides or cleared a fraction? Check for extraneous roots.
4. Inequality sign flips only on × or ÷ by a negative — not on adding a negative.
5. "No solution" = parallel lines (same slope, different intercept); "infinitely many" = the same line.
6. Questions run roughly easy → hard inside a module: bank seconds early to protect the back.
7. Adaptive panic: a hard Module 2 means Module 1 went WELL. Keep going.
8. The reference sheet has gaps (no quadratic formula, no slope) — those are yours to memorise.

✅ TEST-DAY PACING · 考场节奏
1. R&W first (64 min) → 10-min break → Math (70 min)
2. Set the module clock: 22 Q in 35 min, checkpoint at Q11 = 17 min
3. Stuck > 90 s → flag, guess, move on; come back with leftover time
4. Desmos for graphs & systems; type equations, read the intersection
5. Last 2 minutes: every flagged question gets an answer — no blanks

✎ Never leave a blank. Flag & move. Read what is ASKED ✎
